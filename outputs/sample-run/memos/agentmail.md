## 20-second view

AgentMail is an API-first email provider that gives AI agents their own inboxes, positioning itself as the 'Gmail for agents.' It targets developers building email-capable agents, with early customers like Lanesurf and Alchemy reporting high-volume usage. The company is a YC S25 startup with a clear product and some adoption signals, but faces risks of being a horizontal infrastructure layer with unclear defensibility and limited evidence of durable traction.

## Why this matters

AgentMail fits the thesis as an AI-native B2B infrastructure company enabling workflow automation through email agents. It targets a clear technical buyer (developers) and shows early usage, but it is more of a horizontal enabler than a narrow workflow wedge, and its defensibility against platform absorption is uncertain. The thesis favors companies with clear economic buyers and measurable ROI; AgentMail's value is indirect and may be commoditized.
