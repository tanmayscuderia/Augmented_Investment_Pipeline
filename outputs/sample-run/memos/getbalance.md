## 20-second view

Balance is an AI-native accounting firm for SMBs, using agents to reconcile books in real time with human accountant sign-off. The founders are former CFOs/business owners targeting a massive labor-cost category, but public evidence of traction is thin and mostly self-reported. It fits the thesis as a narrow workflow wedge with a clear economic buyer, but defensibility and adoption remain unproven.

## Why this matters

Balance directly targets a recurring, high-labor-cost workflow (monthly bookkeeping) with a clear economic buyer (SMBs already paying for accounting). The team claims domain depth as former CFOs, aligning with the thesis's preference for technical founders with relevant experience. However, the product's reliance on foundation models and lack of independent traction evidence raise concerns about differentiation and defensibility.
