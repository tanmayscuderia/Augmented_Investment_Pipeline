## 20-second view

Trope is an AI-native platform that deploys custom AI agents into ERPs for mid-market companies, aiming to turn the labor-intensive last mile of ERP implementation from services into software. The founding team is technical with relevant deployment experience, but there is no public evidence of customers, revenue, or independent validation yet. The core risk is whether the company can escape a services-heavy model and demonstrate scalable, recurring software value.

## Why this matters

Trope fits the thesis's focus on AI-native workflow automation by targeting a high-labor, recurring workflow (ERP implementation and ongoing management) with a clear economic buyer (mid-market companies already spending on ERP services). However, it conflicts with the thesis's penalty for services disguised as software and raises questions about whether the 'AI FDE' model is truly productized or just a services wrapper. The narrow wedge into ERP customization could expand into a system of action, but evidence of adoption is missing.
