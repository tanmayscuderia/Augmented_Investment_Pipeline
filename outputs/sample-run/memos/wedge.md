## 20-second view

Wedge is a YC S25 startup positioning itself as an 'operating system' for healthcare AI agents, deploying forward-deployed engineers into hospitals to build and maintain custom agentic back-office automations. The team is small and the product is early, with no public evidence of customers or revenue, and the model resembles high-touch services rather than scalable software.

Wedge targets a thesis-relevant workflow (healthcare back-office automation) with a clear economic buyer (hospitals/health plans) and measurable ROI framing.
